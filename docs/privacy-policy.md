# NEXORA: Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 2026  
**Product:** NEXORA Desktop Application  
**Commitment:** Privacy by Default  

---

## 1. Our Privacy Philosophy

NEXORA was engineered from the ground up to respect user autonomy and privacy:
- **We do not collect your personal documents, pictures, videos, or financial files.**
- **We do not collect or transmit your browser passwords, saved credentials, or cookies.**
- **We do not track your web browsing activity or search queries.**
- **We do not run background surveillance telemetry or silent telemetry services.**
- **We do not sell, rent, or monetize user data.**

---

## 2. Information Handled Locally on Your Device

All filesystem scans, duplicate detection calculations, and startup application configurations are processed **100% locally on your computer**:
- **Temporary Files & Caches:** Identified solely for your review and explicit cleaning authorization.
- **Quarantine Staging:** Staged files remain in an encrypted/isolated folder inside your local user profile (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\NEXORA\Quarantine`).
- **Activity History:** Past scan logs, clean records, and custom folder exclusions are saved locally in an on-device store (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\NEXORA\data\nexora_store.json`).

---

## 3. Optional Diagnostics & Feedback

If you choose to submit feedback or report a bug through the **Beta & Telemetry Vault**:
- **Strict Opt-In:** You control whether diagnostic metadata is attached.
- **Sanitized Metadata:** If opted in, diagnostics include strictly: OS name and build, CPU architecture, total RAM, app version, and record counts.
- **Zero Document Leakage:** No usernames, file paths, or private contents are ever captured or transmitted.
- **Transparency:** You can inspect the exact raw JSON metadata before clicking submit.
