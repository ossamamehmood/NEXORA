Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Product: NEXORA PC Maintenance Suite

1. Core Commitments

NEXORA was engineered from the ground up to respect user autonomy and privacy. We do not collect your personal documents, pictures, videos, or financial files. We do not collect or transmit your browser passwords, saved credentials, or cookies. We do not track your web browsing activity or search queries. We run zero background surveillance telemetry.

2. Local-Only Processing

All filesystem scans, duplicate detection calculations, and startup application configurations are processed 100% locally on your computer. Quarantined files remain on your device in your local AppData directory and are never uploaded anywhere.

3. Optional Feedback & Telemetry

If you voluntarily submit feedback via the in-app Beta Feedback Vault, diagnostic metadata is strictly opt-in and sanitized. You can inspect the exact raw JSON metadata before sending. No usernames, file paths, or private contents are ever captured or transmitted.
